SpeedTest Pro is an online internet speed testing website designed to help users understand their internet connection performance.

What is Download Speed?

Download speed measures how quickly your device receives data from the internet. It is important for streaming, browsing and downloading files.

What is Upload Speed?

Upload speed measures how quickly your device sends data to the internet. It is useful for video calls, uploading files and live streaming.

What is Ping?

Ping measures the response time of your internet connection. Lower ping is generally better for online gaming and real-time communication.
